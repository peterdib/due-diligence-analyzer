# The Handyman Group — Key Facts (extracted)

## System / Franchisor

- Franchisor: Local Handyman USA Corp., a Florida corporation, Plantation, FL. Incorporated
  March 26, 2025. Began offering franchises in the US in April 2025 — **the US franchise
  system is under 18 months old.**
- Predecessor/affiliate: The Local Handyman Group Ltd. (British Columbia, Canada) — offered
  franchises in Canada since Oct 2017, in the US since Oct 2023. All US franchisees were
  assigned from the Canadian predecessor to the new Florida entity in April 2025. The Canadian
  predecessor guarantees Local Handyman USA Corp.'s performance obligations (Exhibit F/H
  Guaranty).
- Item 3 (Litigation): none disclosed. Item 4 (Bankruptcy): none disclosed.

## Item 20 — System size and track record

- FDD table (through July 31, 2025): 4 total outlets (3 franchised + 1 area representative),
  up from 1 outlet at start of 2023. Zero terminations, non-renewals, or transfers disclosed.
- **Attachment 1 (supplemental, as of Nov 29, 2025):** 19 more outlets opened (Missouri,
  Nevada, Tennessee, Pennsylvania) and 26 more Franchise Agreements signed but not yet
  opened (Arkansas, Colorado, Michigan, Ohio, Oklahoma, North Carolina, Tennessee) — i.e.
  roughly **~23 open + ~26 in the pipeline ≈ 49 territories sold in under 8 months** of US
  operation. Very rapid, front-loaded growth with essentially no operating history behind it.
- Item 20 Table 5 (projected openings) shows 36 more franchised outlets and 19 signed-not-open
  projected for the next fiscal year, concentrated in new states (Oklahoma, North Carolina,
  Arkansas, South Carolina, Florida, Colorado, Michigan) — an aggressive multi-state expansion
  plan for a system this young.

## Item 5/6/7 — Fees and initial investment (single Base Territory, ~125,000 people)

- Initial Franchise Fee: $60,000 for a Base Territory (~125,000 people); +$0.30/person for
  territory expansion up to a 175,000-person cap. Reduced to $50,000/territory (3 territories)
  or $45,000/territory (4+) for multi-unit signings. 15% veteran's discount available.
- Royalty: tiered 10% (up to $250K annualized Gross Sales) down to 6% (over $1M), recalculated
  each rolling 3-month "Period." **Minimum Annual Royalty Requirement of $10,000/yr** regardless
  of actual sales.
- Franchise Support Engine Fee: $1,500/mo for the first territory (covers QuickBooks,
  Google Ads management, HouseCall Pro access, up to 5 users) + $350/mo per additional
  territory. Can increase up to 10%/year at franchisor's discretion.
- Brand Development Fund: up to 2% of Gross Sales — not currently charged, but franchisor
  reserves the right to start charging it at any time.
- Total Estimated Initial Investment: **$83,000 – $131,300** (Table A: $83,000–$116,300 for a
  Base Territory only; Table B adds up to $75,000 Initial Franchise Fee for an expanded
  territory).
- Transfer fee: 25% of the then-current Initial Franchise Fee. Renewal fee: $5,000.
- Non-compliance fee: $150–$1,000/occurrence. Interest on overdue amounts: 18%/year.

## Item 19 — Financial Performance Representation

**None.** The franchisor makes no financial performance representations of any kind — no
historical unit sales, no revenue ranges, nothing. With only ~4 outlets having any real
operating history as of the FDD data date, there is no way to independently estimate unit
economics from the disclosure document itself.

## Item 21 — Financial Statements (the numbers that matter most here)

Two sets of audited financials are attached, and they tell different stories:

- **Local Handyman USA Corp. (the actual US franchisor, 4-month stub period ending July 31,
  2025):** Total assets $551,024; total liabilities $507,856; shareholders' equity **$43,168**.
  Net income $43,168 on revenue of $205,448 — but $201,162 of that revenue (98%) is upfront
  **franchise fee income**, not royalties (royalty income was only $2,911). The entity is
  brand-new, thinly capitalized, and its apparent profitability is a function of selling new
  territories, not of the franchise system generating recurring royalty income yet.
- **The Local Handyman Group Ltd. (Canadian predecessor/affiliate, the entity that
  guarantees the US franchisor's obligations):** Total deficit (negative equity) of
  **$(1,211,305)** as of July 31, 2025, worsening from $(886,145) in FY2024. Net loss
  **$(342,489)** in FY2025, up from a $(189,145) loss in FY2024 — losses are widening, not
  narrowing. Working capital deficit of $571,223 (FY2024: $363,066), per the capital
  management note. No formal "going concern" qualification was issued by the auditor, but
  **the entity backstopping the franchisor's performance guaranty is itself insolvent on a
  balance-sheet basis and losing more money each year.**

## Territory (Item 12) — no exclusivity, similar structure to other franchise deals reviewed

- Franchisee does **not** receive an exclusive territory in the ordinary sense — franchisor
  reserves broad rights ("Reserved Rights") to operate outside the territory, acquire/merge
  with competing businesses, sell via other channels/trademarks inside the territory, and run
  a Corporate Accounts program in the territory without compensating the franchisee.
- Franchisor may solicit/accept orders inside the franchisee's territory through other
  channels or trademarks, uncompensated.
- "Open Area" concept allows some out-of-territory servicing within a 10-mile radius, but that
  area can be converted to another franchisee's "Assigned Area" at any time, at which point the
  franchisee must hand over all customer records for that area and stop servicing it.

## Data / systems access and other notable Franchise Agreement terms

- Franchisor "will have independent access to all of the information and data that is
  electronically collected and stored on your computer systems" (HouseCall Pro, QuickBooks) —
  **no contractual limits on franchisor's access to franchisee's business/customer data.**
- Personal guaranty required from owners and spouses ("Owner and Spouse Agreement and
  Guaranty") — personal liability survives even if the franchise entity fails.
- Franchise term: 10 years, renewable up to three 5-year terms (15 years max renewal), but
  renewal terms may differ materially from the original agreement.
- Post-termination non-compete: 24 months, covering the territory, a 25-mile radius around it,
  and any other Handyman Group territory.
- Dispute resolution: mandatory mediation then binding arbitration in Manatee County, FL;
  Florida law governs.
- Franchisor holds a right of first refusal to acquire the franchisee's business on any sale.
