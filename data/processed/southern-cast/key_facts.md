- **Equity dropped ~$262k from FY2022 to FY2023** despite a positive (if small) net income both
  years — implies owner distributions well in excess of earnings in FY2023. Confirm actual
  distribution amounts; this matters for normalized-earnings/add-back analysis in a sale.
- **The Line of Credit balance is identical to the penny across FY2023, FY2024, and FY2025**
  ($405,439.63) — worth confirming directly whether this is a static, effectively non-revolving
  balance (e.g., interest-only, not being paid down) or a data artifact; ask for the loan
  agreement and current payoff terms.
- Total assets and equity have both trended down every year in this data (FY2022 → FY2025),
  even in the profitable FY2024.

## Red flag: long-standing "Fraudulent Activity" receivable

- **1127 · Fraudulent Activity Rec** appears on the balance sheet starting FY2023 at $27,002.67,
  drops to $12,602.01 by FY2024, and is **still sitting on the books unchanged at $12,602.01
  through Dec 2025** — over two years without resolution or write-off.
- This account name implies embezzlement, check fraud, or similar loss. It is not explained in
  any document provided. **This needs a direct, specific explanation from ownership**: what
  happened, is it still being pursued (insurance claim, legal action, employee terminated), and
  why is the ~$12.6k residual still carried as a collectible asset rather than written off.

| 6140 · General Supervision | $354,136.38 (8.5%) | $388,320.22 (8.4%) | $330,114.00 (7.9%) | **$663,035.66 (20.0%)** |

In the same year revenue fell 21% and the company posted a net loss, this line **more than
doubled** as a share of income (7.9% → 20.0%). Get a breakdown of what's in this account
(officer salaries, bonuses, distributions run through payroll) — it materially affects
normalized/add-back EBITDA and could indicate either compensation restructuring ahead of a sale
or a one-time bonus/distribution event.
