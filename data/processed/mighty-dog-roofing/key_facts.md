slide deck, "Do Not Distribute" watermark on every page).

**This is a franchisor sales/"Discovery Process" presentation, not the FDD.** It is prepared by
Don Schneider, Director of Franchise Development for HorsePower Brands (the parent company),
and it references Item 7 and Item 19 figures but does not include the actual FDD tables,

## System / Franchisor

- Brand: Mighty Dog Roofing®, launched Nov 2020. One of ~15 brands under HorsePower Brands
  (a multi-brand franchisor platform — other brands include Blingle!, iFOAM, Heroes Lawn Care,
  Gatsby Glass, Groovy Hues, Bumble Bee Blinds, Stand Strong Fencing, plus internal "Zee"
  service brands: ZeeRecruit, ZeeBOOKS, ZeeLOGIC, ZeeMarketing, ZeePartnership, ZeeSupply,
  ZeeBenefits, ZeeUniversity, ZeeFleet, Varsity Zone, Howie).
- System size claimed: 200+ territories awarded, 80+ franchisees, average 3.1 territories per
  franchisee, "several multi-Horsepower-Brand franchisees." No date given for these figures, and
  no Item 20 table (openings/closures/transfers/terminations by year) is included in this deck.
- Press claims: ranked #1 Roofing Franchise by Entrepreneur "for 5 consecutive years"; #1 New &
  Emerging Franchisor 2023 (out of 3,000+); #11 fastest-growing franchise in the world 2023;
  Top 100 Roofing Contractor ranking climbing from #90 (2022) to #49 (2023) to #20 (2024).
- Item 3 (Litigation) / Item 4 (Bankruptcy): not addressed anywhere in this deck — needs the
  actual FDD.

## Business model

- Sub-contractor labor model: franchisee does sales/marketing only; all installation labor is
  1099 sub-contractors (required to carry their own licenses/insurance), sourced partly through
  a national partnership with SRS Distribution.
- Ownership models: "Semi-Involved" (owner 20-30 hrs/wk + a General Manager runs day-to-day) or
  "Owner Operator" (owner 40-50 hrs/wk, often acts as GM initially). **Absentee ownership is not
  allowed** under either model.
- Minimum staffing required to start: GM/Sales Manager + 2 Sales Representatives (Owner-Operator
  model requires 2 Sales Reps to start regardless).
- Required sales vehicle: white Dodge Ram (or similar) crew cab, 1500/2500.
- Office space is optional — can work from home to start; if leased, must be within the
  protected territory.
- Core services: roof replacement/repair (asphalt, metal standing seam, concrete/clay tile,
  shake, membrane); add-on services: gutters, siding, windows, commercial — framed explicitly as
  upsell/ticket-size boosters.
- Supplementing service (optional, franchisor-run): recovers insurance-adjuster-missed line
  items on claims for a flat $75–$200/claim fee.

## Industry data cited (IBISWorld, uncredited elsewhere)

- Roofing industry: $51.3B. Cited as "least trusted of all home services," "largest average
  ticket item on the home," expected to grow over 5 years. Top 4 enterprises hold <5% of
  industry revenue (fragmented, no dominant competitor).
- Product segmentation: Asphalt 42%, Single-Ply 25%, Other 21%, Sheet Metal 12%.
- Market segmentation: Existing Commercial 32%, Existing Residential 31%, New Commercial 25%,
  New Residential 12%.

## Territory and Royalty

- Territory unit: 50,000 Owner-Occupied Homes = 1 territory = $59,500 franchise fee.
- Territory fee scale (larger territories, presumably multiples, at a discount to linear
  scaling):

  | Territory size (owner-occupied homes) | Franchise fee |
  |---|---|
  | 50,000 | $59,500 |
  | 100,000 | $99,500 |
  | 150,000 | $134,500 |
  | 200,000 | $164,500 |
  | 250,000 | $194,500 |

- Royalty: starts at 6%, scales down to 2% as revenue increases (thresholds not given in this

  cover the first 12 months of royalty.
- Multiple territories can be run from one central location.

## Training and support

- Onboarding: 12-20 week, two-phase process (Phase 1: entity building, business prep, biweekly
  calls, 0-13 weeks, or 0-4 weeks if self-funded; Phase 2: 7-week "Insider/University" online
  curriculum — vendor intros, systems/technology, hiring/training/retention, marketing, product
  knowledge).
- Franchise Business Coach (FBC): one assigned per franchisee, ratio no worse than 1 FBC per 20
  franchisees; runs weekly/monthly business reviews, quarterly in-market training, on-site for
  all launches.
- Sales development: dedicated sales coach, weekly webinars, regional meetings, on-site "boots
  on the ground" sales training.
- Technology stack: AI-powered drone roof inspection/damage detection ("Drone Super Scan"), an
  in-house digital sales/visualizer tool (partnered with Owens Corning for a "Curb Appeal
  Visualizer"), Hail Trace storm/hail tracking, a door-to-door canvassing/territory-management
  app. Marketing/tech fees are bundled into Item 7's Technology Fee (not broken out in this
  deck — get the actual monthly amount from the FDD).
- Owens Corning "Platinum Preferred" status: claimed <1% of roofing contractors hold it —
  rebates (3% quarterly + 1% annually), enhanced warranties, direct-lead access.
