# Mighty Dog Roofing — Key Facts (extracted)

Source: `data/raw/mighty-dog-roofing/Mighty Dog Roofing 2026 FDD Issued 4.20.26, updated 5.19.26.pdf`
(the actual issued FDD — 200 pages, 881K extracted chars, no OCR needed) and
`data/raw/mighty-dog-roofing/2026 Mighty Dog Roofing Unit Economics.xlsx` (franchisor-provided
workbook containing embedded FDD Item 19 screenshots and a supplementary percentile-based
profitability breakdown not found elsewhere). The 48-page sales presentation reviewed previously
is now superseded by this disclosure-grade data. Franchisor entity: **MDR United LLC** d/b/a
Mighty Dog Roofing, part of the HorsePower Brands platform.

## System / Franchisor

- Brand launched Nov 2020. 74 total franchisees, 329 Protected Territories as of Dec 31, 2025.
- Outlet count history (Item 20, Table 1 — each Protected Territory = 1 "Outlet"):

  | Year | Start | Opened | Terminations | Non-Renewals | End | Net Change |
  |---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
  | 2023 | 232 | 147 | 15 | 0 | 364 | +132 |
  | 2024 | 364 | 75 | 36 | 0 | 403 | +39 |
  | 2025 | 403 | 26 | **100** | 0 | 329 | **-74** |

  **This is the single biggest finding in this FDD.** After two years of rapid growth, the system
  lost 74 net outlets in 2025 (-18.4%), driven by 100 terminations (vs. 15 in 2023 and 36 in
  2024) against only 26 new openings — a near-total collapse in new territory sales alongside an
  acceleration in terminations. Roughly 1 in 4 outlets that existed at the start of 2025 was
  terminated during the year.
- An independent franchisee association — **Mighty Dog® Franchise Owners (MDFO)**, a chapter of
  the American Association of Franchisees & Dealers (AAFD) — asked to be included in this
  disclosure document. Independent AAFD chapters typically form when franchisees feel they need
  organized representation against the franchisor; worth asking directly what prompted MDFO's
  formation.
- Item 3 (Litigation) — **9 pending actions**, none concluded, none in prior fiscal years'
  history beyond what's listed:
  - **4 franchisor-initiated actions** (all filed Aug–Oct 2025 in E.D. Pa.) against departed
    franchisees for breach of non-compete/restrictive covenants, unpaid royalties, and
    misappropriation, seeking **$799,701–$1,350,290 each in "lost future royalties"** plus
    injunctive relief. All 4 defendant groups filed counterclaims alleging fraudulent/negligent
    misrepresentation and unfair trade practices; MDR United has moved to dismiss each.
  - **5 separate franchisee-initiated actions** (Nov 2025–Mar 2026, filed in FL, NE, PA, NJ, and
    NC by different multi-plaintiff groups — at least 9 distinct franchisee entities across
    these 5 suits) alleging **fraudulent misrepresentation, negligent misrepresentation, breach
    of contract**, and various state deceptive-trade-practices/franchise acts. All seek
    **rescission of their franchise agreements** plus damages. MDR United denies the allegations.
  - This litigation pattern (franchisor suing exiting franchisees for going to competitors while
    a wave of other franchisees sue back alleging they were misled into signing) is a coherent
    and serious pattern, not isolated incidents, and lines up with the termination spike above.
- Item 4 (Bankruptcy): none required to be disclosed.

## Item 5/7 — Fees and Initial Investment (confirmed against the actual FDD table)

- Franchise fee scale matches the sales deck exactly: $59,500 (1 territory/50,000 owner-occupied
  homes) up to $194,500 (5 territories/250,000 homes). Overage fee: **$1.19 per home** above the
  territory's contracted owner-occupied-home count.
- **Full Item 7 table for a single territory reconciles the sales-deck gap identified previously**
  — Total: **$173,672 – $225,816**, itemized across 19 line items: Initial Fee $59,500, Insurance
  $4,500–10,000, Tuition Fee $4,995, Travel/Training $1,500–3,500, Opening Package
  $12,150–14,580, Rent & Utilities $200–2,500, Vehicles $15,366–17,670, Licenses/Permits
  $3,200–10,000, Professional Fees $1,000–10,500, Technology Fee $2,076/mo, Special Software Fee
  $900/mo, Contact Center Fee $850–1,660/mo, Dues & Subscriptions $3,000–3,500, Brand Marketing
  Fee $15,500, Initial/Local Marketing $20,000, Digital Management Fee $1,500/mo, Accounting
  Services Fee $2,435/mo, ZeePartnerships Fee $5,000, Additional Funds $20,000–60,000.
- Minimum Royalty: $500/month starting on the 1-year anniversary (franchisor may raise up to 10%/yr
  after year 5).

## Item 12 — Territory

- **No exclusive territory** — franchisor reserves the right to permit another qualified party to
  open a Mighty Dog Roofing location in your Protected Territory if you don't meet performance
  thresholds; also reserves National/Regional Account ("NORA") rights to solicit and service
  chain/multi-location customers within your territory without your consent.
- Franchisor's **Contact Center controls appointment assignment** within your territory —
  appointments are not guaranteed to be routed to you even inside your own territory.
- Relocation requires franchisor consent; if >5% of Gross Revenue comes from outside your
  Protected Territory, franchisor can require you to buy an additional franchise (no right of
  first refusal is granted in exchange).

## Item 17 — Renewal, Termination, Transfer

- 10-year initial term, one 10-year renewal option. Renewal requires a fee equal to 20% of the
  then-current Initial Franchise Fee, full compliance history, and executing the then-current
  (potentially materially different) franchise agreement.
- All owners/members/shareholders and spouses must sign personal guaranties (Exhibit A).
- Governing law: Pennsylvania. Disputes go through AAA mediation then arbitration in Bucks
  County, PA — except franchisor may seek injunctions without bond (used repeatedly per Item 3).

**This confirms the exact red flag flagged as a risk before the FDD arrived**: the average
($1,468,568, the only figure in the sales deck) is 52% higher than the median ($963,683) — only

**Table 2 — Segment mix:** Residential 84.0% ($86.4M), Commercial 16.0% ($16.4M) of $102.8M total

**Not disclosed anywhere in Item 19 or elsewhere in the FDD: cost structure, EBITDA, or
owner-benefit/profit data.** Gross Sales is revenue only — no royalty, subcontractor labor,
marketing spend, or overhead is netted out.

## Item 21 — Franchisor Financial Statements (MDR United LLC, audited, FY2025 vs FY2024)

| | FY2025 | FY2024 | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Total Revenue | $13,639,138 | $14,205,446 | -4.0% |
| — Royalties (tracks system health directly) | $7,108,012 | $8,307,137 | **-14.4%** |
| — Franchise fees | $4,490,924 | $3,425,936 | +31.1% |
| Net Income | $2,176,027 | $5,310,710 | **-59.0%** |
| Members' Distributions paid out | $3,545,000 | $2,773,530 | +27.8% |
| Members' Deficit (negative equity) | **$(2,043,677)** | $(674,704) | Worsening |

MDR United LLC has run a **negative members' equity position in both years shown**, and the
deficit deepened by $1.37M in 2025 — driven by owners distributing $3.545M in 2025 against only
$2.18M of net income (a $1.37M cash outflow beyond earnings, in the same year royalty revenue
fell 14% and 100 outlets terminated). Franchise-fee revenue rose even as terminations spiked and
new openings collapsed, likely reflecting recognition of previously deferred fees (Deferred
Franchise Fee Revenue liability fell from ~$10.5M to ~$7.0M) rather than new territory sales.

## Business model (confirmed, unchanged from sales deck review)

- Sub-contractor labor model; franchisee handles sales/marketing/QC only. No exclusive territory,
  as above. Absentee ownership not allowed. Required sales vehicle, drone inspection tech, Owens
  Corning Platinum Preferred status — all as previously summarized from the sales deck and not
  contradicted by the FDD.
