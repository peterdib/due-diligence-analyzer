## Item 5/6/7 — Fees and initial investment (Tier 1 territory, single territory)

- Initial Franchise Fee: $56,500 (Tier 1: up to 400 registered aircraft / 25 aviation businesses).
  Tier 2: $49,500 (up to 300 aircraft / 15 businesses).
- Overage: if actual territory exceeds the tier cap, +$25/aircraft over cap and +$125/business
  over cap, capped at $10,000/territory extra.
  - **Winston-Salem territory (OPT_NC2 Tier 1) reported 581 aircraft and 37 aviation
    businesses** — both over the Tier 1 cap (400 / 25). Estimated overage: (581-400)×$25 =
    $4,525, plus (37-25)×$125 = $1,500 → ~$6,025 extra, for an effective initial franchise fee
    around **$62,500** rather than the headline $56,500. Confirm exact figure with franchisor.
- Other required initial fees: Custom Marketing & Services Activation Fee $5,000; Software
  Implementation Assistance Fee $2,500; One-Time Brand Fund Contribution $7,500/territory.
- Ongoing Royalty: 7.5% of Gross Sales, with a minimum royalty floor regardless of sales
  (see table below).
- Ongoing Brand Fund contribution: 1.5% of Gross Sales, franchisor reserves right to raise to 3%.
- Total Estimated Initial Investment (single territory): **$225,685 – $417,806** (per FDD Item 7 /
  workbook). Required net worth $250,000, liquid capital $100,000 (per candidate presentation).

## Territory (Item 12) — no exclusivity

- Franchisee does **not** receive an exclusive territory. Franchisor can grant other franchisees
  or company outlets rights in/near the territory (including "the same airport"), operate
  national/regional accounts in the territory without compensating the franchisee, and reserves
  rights if the franchisee is in default or can't meet demand.

## Notable contractual terms flagged in Jeff's annotations (`RealClean Aircraft FDD Jeff Comments_ANNOTATIONS.txt`)

- Personal Guaranty required from all owners (Exhibit B) — personal liability survives even if
  the LLC fails; attorney review strongly recommended.
- Franchisor claims complete ownership of and unrestricted access to all business data
  (sales, customer data, QuickBooks), with no contractual limitation.
- Franchisee may not use generative AI tools in the business without franchisor's written
  approval — broad restriction covering marketing, customer comms, planning, analysis.
- Mandatory supplier list (Zeitler Enterprises for products, Conric for Brand Fund services) —
  franchisor admits it receives payments from designated suppliers based on franchisee
  purchases (rebates/kickbacks), and controls pricing on required purchases.
- Subscription-model administrative fee (for a planned cross-territory subscription product) is
  left completely undefined — no cap, no formula disclosed yet.
- Customer-review non-compliance fee: $90/week (up to $250/week after 12 weeks) if review
  ratings fall below 4.0 stars / 80% satisfaction on franchisor-designated platforms.
- Must open within 4 months of signing the Franchise Agreement or franchisor can terminate —
  aggressive timeline for a system with only ~1 year of franchisee track record.
- Renewal (additional 10-year term) may come with materially different terms than the original
  agreement.
- Confidentiality clauses restrict current/former franchisees from discussing their experience
  with prospective franchisees — limits how much a candidate can learn from existing owners.
- Primary office must be located within the franchisee's own territory (can't home-base just
  outside it, e.g. in a nearby city not in the territory).
- ~100+ hours of initial training; only 3 individuals trained free (typically Operating
  Principal, Manager, Lead Detailer); incidental travel/lodging costs are additional.
